# Writing with Bloom

Bloom continues the prose before your caret, alongside any examples you choose. The context view shows what was supplied and any omissions. Text after the caret stays out.

If you get a blurb, a review or an unrelated new passage, try placing the caret inside an unfinished sentence. Continuations can still wander; keep what serves your writing.

For example, explore after:

> Leo looked at Inez. She held out her hand for

Choose **Writing → Explore continuations**, then read the alternatives as possibilities. Keep a word, a small beat or an image; accept a whole passage when it suits the manuscript. Branch from a continuation when you want to follow it further. Generated text enters your manuscript through explicit acceptance, and native Undo lets you reverse it.

Choose **Writing → Writing examples…** to supply passages before the manuscript. Try prose whose voice you want to explore, then inspect the actual context. Examples can also pull a continuation away from the present scene.

Start with **Writing → Variation → Default**. This is the Standard sampling setting. **Less** uses lower randomness; **More (experimental)** uses wider sampling. None guarantees continuity.

Replay uses the captured prompt, model, settings and seeds. Later edits do not rewrite that saved context.
